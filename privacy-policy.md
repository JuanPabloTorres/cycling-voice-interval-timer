# Privacy Policy for RidePulse

**Last Updated: January 22, 2026**

## Overview

RidePulse ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our interval timer application for cycling.

## Information Collection and Use

**RidePulse does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.**

All data created and used by RidePulse remains exclusively on your device. We have no access to your information.

## Data Stored Locally

RidePulse stores the following data locally on your device:

- **Training Plans**: Custom interval training plans you create
- **Timer Events**: Work intervals, rest periods, warmup, and cooldown configurations
- **Voice Settings**: Your preferred voice, speech rate, pitch, and volume settings
- **App Preferences**: General application settings

This data is stored using:
- **SQLite Database**: For training plans and timer events
- **Local Storage**: For application preferences

All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

## Permissions

RidePulse requests the following permissions to function properly:

### Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
- **Purpose**: Display the running timer in your device's notification bar
- **Usage**: Shows elapsed time and current interval while the app runs in the background
- **Data Access**: No personal data is accessed or transmitted

### Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE)
- **Purpose**: Keep the timer running when the app is in the background
- **Usage**: Ensures accurate timing and voice announcements during your workout
- **Data Access**: No personal data is accessed or transmitted

### Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK)
- **Purpose**: Keep the screen on during active training sessions
- **Usage**: Prevents the screen from turning off while you're viewing the timer
- **Data Access**: No personal data is accessed or transmitted

## Third-Party Services

RidePulse does NOT use any third-party services, including:
- No analytics or tracking services
- No advertising networks
- No cloud storage services
- No social media integrations
- No crash reporting tools

## Text-to-Speech (TTS)

RidePulse uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) system to provide voice guidance. Voice synthesis is performed entirely on your device using your operating system's TTS engine. No audio data is recorded, transmitted, or stored.

## Data Security

Since all data remains on your device:
- Your data is protected by your device's security measures
- We cannot access your data
- Data backup and restore depend on your device's backup settings
- Uninstalling the app will delete all locally stored data

## Children's Privacy

RidePulse does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is designed as a fitness tool and does not contain features that appeal specifically to children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## Data Deletion

To delete all data stored by RidePulse:
1. Uninstall the application from your device
2. All locally stored training plans and settings will be permanently deleted

Alternatively, you can clear app data through your device settings:
- **Android**: Settings → Apps → RidePulse → Storage → Clear Data

## Your Rights

Since RidePulse does not collect or store any personal information on external servers:
- There is no data to access, modify, or delete from our systems
- All data control is in your hands through your device
- You maintain complete ownership of all data created in the app
